# TENTOESDOWN

TENTOESDOWN is an alternate-history Hearts of Iron IV campaign centered on an independent Indiana in 1936.

After one common focus, the player chooses among four governments:

- McNutt's Indiana Democrats
- the Harrison Regency
- Pelley's Silver State
- Hapgood's Wabash Congress

Each government has one temporary consolidation category, five operations, and a 70 Political Control threshold. McNutt wins a convention, Harrison builds a regency, Pelley takes the Statehouse from inside, and Hapgood prepares for civil war.

The transfer of power is only the opening. The new government spends 1936-37 restoring departmental authority, ending the Vigo occupation, and tracing the Chicago payroll note. It then decides how much to prepare before provoking a limited federal battle at Hammond.

After that confrontation, normally in 1938, one compact tree opens into Purdue science; Indiana railways and manufacturers; Culver, Fort Harrison, and Guard reform; Special Services and diplomacy; and a government-specific institutional settlement. The campaign contains 121 focuses, but unchosen governments are hidden and the continuous-focus panel sits below all story content.
