# TENTOESDOWN - Roadmap

## Current playable campaign

- One common opening focus and four explicit governments.
- One temporary political category per government, with five operations and a 70 Political Control threshold.
- McNutt's convention, the Harrison regency, Pelley's internal seizure, and Hapgood's prepared civil war.
- Three post-succession files that can be handled in the player's preferred order.
- A three-step investigation of Consortium Credit with four government-specific outcomes.
- Optional preparations for a limited federal border conflict at Hammond.
- Victory, defeat, and cancellation handling for the federal conflict.
- A four-year United States penalty after federal defeat.
- A compact 121-focus tree with the continuous-focus panel below all campaign content.
- Post-emergency mini-arcs for Purdue instruments and atomic research; Boone County power, railways, manufacturers, and Peru logistics; Culver, Fort Harrison, the 38th, Atterbury, Baer Field, and Jefferson Proving Ground; Special Services and diplomacy.
- One hidden-until-chosen eight-focus institutional arc for each government.
- Actual country-leader hover biographies for all four rulers.

## Next expansion target

The next build should extend the new endpoints into the 1940-42 global campaign:

1. Turn the Purdue isotope committee into an expensive, contested reactor and materials program rather than an event-delivered weapon.
2. Give each government a foreign-policy doctrine and wartime cabinet that grows from its completed institutional settlement.
3. Convert the Hoosier General Staff, Baer Field, and Indiana Arsenal into distinctive divisions, air formations, equipment choices, and commanders.
4. Let the federal defeat damage United States readiness through investigations, appropriations failures, command turnover, and recovery decisions rather than a single unexplained modifier.
5. Give the Office of Strategic Inquiry operations with concrete targets and consequences.
6. Add commissioned focus art and portraits where custom art materially improves navigation or character recognition.

## Development rules

- Respect the campaign calendar. Do not compress multi-year state formation into 1936.
- Build each mini-arc vertically: focus, event, decision if needed, AI behavior, effects, prose, and live test.
- A focus earns its place through information, position, preparation, or consequence. It does not need to flip a flag or erase a spirit.
- Use temporary decision categories only when they clarify a live contest.
- Do not add another permanent dashboard while focuses, events, or one short category can carry the same choice.
