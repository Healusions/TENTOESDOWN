# Research Ledger

## McNutt government and the Two Percent Club

- Paul V. McNutt became governor in 1933 and used executive reorganization to centralize Indiana administration.
- Frank McHale was a principal organizer in McNutt's political Machine.
- State employees were expected to join the Hoosier Democratic Club and contribute two percent of salary.

Status: historical foundation. Exact meetings and documents are dramatized.

## Wendell Willkie

- Willkie was born in Elwood, Indiana, in 1892 and studied law at Indiana University.
- He led Commonwealth & Southern, a utility holding company, during the 1930s.
- His national reputation grew through the public contest over the Tennessee Valley Authority and utility policy.

Primary Indiana institutional reference:

- https://www.in.gov/history/state-historical-markers/find-a-marker/wendell-willkie/

Status: historical foundation. His 1936 working arrangement with McNutt is alternate history. The active route keeps him independent from McNutt's Machine.

## Terre Haute, 1935

- Federal Labor Union No. 19694 at Columbian Enameling and Stamping participated in the dispute preceding the Terre Haute general strike.
- McNutt declared martial law and deployed the Indiana National Guard.
- Eugene V. Debs's house on North Eighth Street provides a real institutional address for the route.
- Powers Hapgood was a labor organizer and Indiana native.

Status: historical foundation. The Wabash Congress, arms routes, militia names, and civil war are alternate-history inventions.

## Stephenson and Pelley

- D. C. Stephenson's papers and disclosures were tied to the exposure of Indiana Klan political corruption.
- The Indianapolis Times won the 1928 Pulitzer Prize for its campaign against Klan influence.
- William Dudley Pelley founded the Silver Legion in 1933 and operated from Asheville, North Carolina.

Status: historical foundation. Pelley's acquisition and use of a copied county index is alternate history.

## Harrison Crown and the 1816 Society

- The Harrison family connects William Henry Harrison and Benjamin Harrison to Indiana political memory.
- The Columbia Club was a major Indianapolis Republican institution.
- The Indiana Constitution restricted public debt and executive tenure.

Status: mixed. The 1816 Society, Chicago consortium, Continuity Act, regency, and crown are deliberate alternate-history inventions.

## Purdue physics

- Karl Lark-Horovitz transformed Purdue's Physics Department beginning in 1929.
- Purdue decided in 1935 to construct a Van de Graaff accelerator and a cyclotron.
- Hubert Yearian and John Howe began cyclotron magnet construction in 1936.
- Purdue's shops and faculty labor kept construction costs unusually low.

Primary Purdue references:

- https://www.physics.purdue.edu/about/history/lark_horovitz_era.html
- https://www.physics.purdue.edu/about/history/war_period.html

Status: historical foundation. Indiana's use of the program to negotiate the Federal Standoff is alternate history.
