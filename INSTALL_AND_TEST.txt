TENTOESDOWN — FOUR-PATH CONSOLIDATION BUILD

INSTALL

1. Close Hearts of Iron IV.
2. Commit or back up the current TENTOESDOWN folder.
3. Open the supplied ZIP.
4. Open the folder named TENTOESDOWN.
5. Copy everything inside it into the existing TENTOESDOWN mod folder.
6. Merge folders and replace files when Windows asks.
7. Start a new 1936 game. Old saves are not compatible.

This is cumulative. Do not install an older streamlined package first.


FIVE-MINUTE SMOKE TEST

1. Start Indiana on 1 January 1936.

Expected:
- Exactly four starting national spirits.
- No Indiana decision category before a route is chosen.
- An optional two-page briefing.

2. Open the console and enter:

Focus.AutoComplete
Decision.Fast
event IND_debug.1

Choose the test-resources option. It also sets Political Control to 100.

3. Complete A State in Suspension.

Expected:
- Exactly four mutually exclusive political focuses appear.
- The choices are McNutt, Harrison, Pelley, and Hapgood.

4. Choose Keep the Indiana Democrats.

Expected:
- Exactly one Indiana category appears: The State Convention.
- Political Control starts at 10.
- Call the County Chairmen is available immediately.

5. Complete The Machine in Daylight without taking the first decision.

Expected:
- The focus is not blocked by a decision.
- Two additional operations become visible.
- Only one operation can run at a time.

6. Continue through McNutt Holds the Statehouse after reaching 70 Political Control.

Expected:
- McNutt remains leader.
- Wendell Willkie is available as an advisor after Bring Willkie Home.
- The campaign category disappears.
- All four starting crisis spirits remain.

7. Complete The Emergency Outlives the Governor and its three parallel crisis focuses.

Expected:
- Close the Vigo File removes The Wabash Emergency.
- Reissue the Payroll Orders removes A State in Suspension.
- Open the Chicago Ledger removes Consortium Credit and adds the route-specific outcome.
- The Federal Embargo remains.

8. Complete Indiana Signs Its Own Orders and Contest the Freight Holds.

Expected:
- The severe Federal Embargo becomes the lighter Federal Standoff.
- Special Services, Calumet industry, and Purdue content become available only now.

9. Complete Whispers from West Lafayette and The West Lafayette Memorandum.

Expected:
- The first Purdue event reveals the procurement pattern.
- The second event offers a secret state program or supervised university program.
- Either choice removes the Federal Standoff and grants the listed research bonus.

Turn Focus.AutoComplete and Decision.Fast off by entering both commands again.


COMMUNIST ROUTE TEST

Use a fresh 1936 game.

1. Choose The Wabash Answer.
2. Complete all five campaign operations.
3. Complete The Wabash Congress.

Expected:
- Powers Hapgood becomes leader of the communist side.
- The democratic opposition receives Indianapolis and approximately 55% of Indiana, normally two of the four states.
- The Wabash side receives eight militia divisions when every preparation was completed.
- The communist campaign category disappears when the war starts.
- The post-takeover crisis branch remains unavailable until the civil war ends.
- The Wabash Emergency is not silently removed by the change of government.


OTHER ROUTE CHECK

Harrison:
- The Continuity Campaign is the only route category.
- The first credit operation grants one off-map civilian factory.
- The Harrison Regency Council takes power after 70 Political Control.

Pelley:
- The Silver Entry is the only route category.
- The takeover occurs through the Statehouse watch, without a civil war.
- Pelley becomes leader after 70 Political Control.


LOG CHECK

After testing, close the game and inspect:

Documents\Paradox Interactive\Hearts of Iron IV\logs\error.log

Search for IND_, IND., IND_focus, IND_decisions, IND_ideas, and TENTOESDOWN.

The previous build produced character recruitment errors because route leaders were recruited inside events. This package recruits all possible Indiana leaders in country history and only promotes them in events.
