TENTOESDOWN — STREAMLINED REBUILD

INSTALL

1. Close Hearts of Iron IV.
2. Commit or back up the current TENTOESDOWN folder.
3. Open the supplied ZIP.
4. Open the folder named TENTOESDOWN.
5. Copy everything inside it into your existing TENTOESDOWN mod folder.
6. Merge folders and replace files when Windows asks.
7. Start a new 1936 game. Old saves are not compatible.

The package deliberately includes small replacement files for every obsolete event and localization file, so drag-and-drop replacement disables the old systems without requiring manual deletion.

FIVE-MINUTE SMOKE TEST

1. Start Indiana on 1 January 1936.

Expected:
- Exactly four starting national spirits.
- No Indiana decision category before a route is chosen.
- A two-page optional briefing that can be skipped without effects.

2. Open the console and enter:

Focus.AutoComplete
Decision.Fast
event IND_debug.1

Choose “Add test resources.”

3. Complete A State in Suspension.

Expected:
- Exactly three mutually exclusive political focuses appear.
- Harrison, Pelley, and Hapgood are explained directly in their descriptions.

4. Choose The Harrison Offer, then Accounts Behind the Flag.

Expected:
- One category named The Political Crisis appears.
- Only Commit the Guarantors is available.
- Completing it unlocks The Harrison Name.
- The next action replaces the previous one; actions do not accumulate.

5. Continue through An American Crown.

Expected:
- Harrison Regency Council becomes leader.
- Indiana becomes Non-Aligned.
- The Political Crisis category disappears.
- A Government Washington Can Name becomes available.

6. Complete A Government Washington Can Name, Whispers from West Lafayette, and The West Lafayette Memorandum.

Expected:
- Purdue is unavailable before the government handoff.
- The first Purdue event only reveals the program.
- The second event offers two clear settlements.
- Either settlement removes The Federal Standoff and grants the listed nuclear bonus.

Turn Focus.AutoComplete and Decision.Fast off by entering both commands again.

COMMUNIST ROUTE TEST

Use a fresh 1936 game. Follow The Wabash Answer through The Wabash Congress.

Expected:
- Powers Hapgood becomes leader of the Communist side.
- A Democratic opposition receives roughly 35 percent of Indiana.
- The Political Crisis category disappears when war begins.
- A Government Washington Can Name remains locked while the civil war is active.

LOG CHECK

After testing, close the game and inspect:

Documents\Paradox Interactive\Hearts of Iron IV\logs\error.log

Search for IND_, IND., IND_focus, IND_decisions, IND_ideas, and the TENTOESDOWN path. Errors from unrelated enabled mods should be separated from Indiana errors.
