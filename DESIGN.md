# TENTOESDOWN — Design and Writing Guide

## Campaign premise

Indiana begins on 1 January 1936 as a functioning government without a settled constitutional position. The Supreme Court dismantled the New Deal more aggressively, Paul V. McNutt used Indiana's reorganized executive departments to maintain recovery policy, and Franklin Roosevelt's attempt to federalize the Indiana National Guard during the Terre Haute occupation produced the break. Indianapolis requested statutory clarification and never acknowledged the final order.

The opening is not a simulation of every institution created by that crisis. It is a short struggle over which organized faction inherits McNutt's emergency state.

## The three routes

After one common focus, the player makes one explicit and permanent commitment:

1. **The Harrison Crown** — The 1816 Society converts private credit into a Continuity Committee, a Guard-backed regency, and eventually an American monarchy.
2. **The Silver State** — William Dudley Pelley uses copies from D. C. Stephenson's political network, converted county chairmen, and a replaced Statehouse detail to seize power without a civil war.
3. **The Wabash Revolt** — Powers Hapgood organizes Terre Haute, the mine roads, and Calumet labor into a provisional government that must win a civil war.

McNutt remains central to all three. He built the departments each route captures. His defining contradiction is control: he treated every outside institution as something he could ride and created the government through which those institutions can replace him.

## Interface budget

- The player should normally see no more than one major Indiana decision category during the opening.
- The Political Crisis category shows only the next route operation. It has no numerical meters, repeating upkeep, chapters, or reference guide.
- Every route uses the same rhythm: complete a focus, perform one timed operation, receive the next story focus.
- Solving the crisis removes the category permanently.
- Flavor belongs in events and descriptions. Mechanical requirements and consequences belong in tooltips.
- A button must advance the route or change the strategic state. Activity is not content.

## Campaign pacing

- The route is chosen in the first ten weeks.
- The Harrison and Pelley routes take power after three preparations and one decisive focus.
- The communist route begins a civil war after three preparations. Its crisis is not resolved until that war ends.
- The federal embargo becomes a simpler Federal Standoff only after one government can answer for Indiana.
- Purdue's strategic program is completely gated behind that handoff. No whisper, focus, decision, or bonus appears during the political opening or communist civil war.

## Purdue rule

Purdue is leverage, not a magic weapon. The 1935 decision to construct a Van de Graaff accelerator and cyclotron, Karl Lark-Horovitz's department, Hubert Yearian and John Howe's work, and the Purdue Research Foundation provide the historical foundation. Indiana may accelerate the institution and its procurement; it does not receive an atomic bomb or skip the industrial steps required to build one.

The first Purdue event must feel like a procurement irregularity. The second establishes an agreement that ends the administrative standoff with Washington while granting a limited nuclear research advantage.

## Writing register

The prose should sit between a state archive, a newspaper morgue, a political novel, and an intelligence file. It may be stylish, but it must remain concrete.

Every major event must establish:

1. A real room, street, town, institution, publication, or office.
2. A named person or organization with an immediate objective.
3. The fact that changed and justified interrupting gameplay.
4. The action being taken.
5. The office, flag, spirit, unit, cost, or later event that remembers it.

### House rules

- Every description contains at least one checkable proper noun.
- No narrative text refers to the player, a mod, a timeline, development, implementation, or real-world history outside the setting.
- Do not use portent in place of information. Delete empty closers such as “history had begun,” “nothing would be the same,” and “Indiana would remember.”
- Do not use unnamed principals such as “the old men” unless the scene has already identified them.
- Options sound like actions: “Replace the night watch” is better than “A difficult choice.”
- Political extremism is neither sanitized nor rendered as theatrical evil. Pelley's antisemitism, Stephenson's Klan network, political repression, and armed labor conflict are described accurately and without voyeurism.
- Proper nouns are structural: Wabash Avenue, the Columbia Club, North Eighth Street, WFBM, Gary Works, Local 19694, the 38th Division, Purdue, and the Constitution of 1851 make this an Indiana story.

## Character rule

Recurring figures need three layers:

- **Public role** — how supporters, newspapers, and opponents describe them.
- **Institutional leverage** — the money, files, unions, churches, offices, or armed followers they control.
- **Private contradiction** — the loyalty, fear, appetite, or blind spot that prevents them from acting as ideology tokens.

## War rule

War is a payoff, not a substitute for politics. The Wabash civil war is justified because the communist route is built through public organizations and armed networks that cannot capture the Statehouse quietly. Pelley does not receive the same conflict: his route succeeds by infiltrating the Machine's channels. The Harrison route succeeds through finance, legal form, and Guard custody.

Later federal war content must use Indiana's geography, actual divisions, equipment, rail movement, and achievable settlement conditions. It may not resolve solely through event arithmetic.

## Definition of success

After the opening, the player should be able to answer:

- Which faction was chosen?
- Which three acts made its takeover possible?
- Why did that route use a regency, a seizure, or a civil war?
- Which institutions survived the change of government?
- Why did Purdue become available only after the political crisis ended?
