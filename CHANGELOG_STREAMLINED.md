# Meat and Bones Tree Rebuild

## Focus-tree presentation

- Rebuilt the complete geometry around one centered succession crown, three short domestic-file lanes, one Hammond funnel, and five compact post-emergency districts.
- Reduced the visible width to coordinates 0-20 and centered the opening view on the first focus.
- Moved continuous focuses below the last campaign row so the panel no longer covers industry or military content.
- Made all four government mini-trees reuse one footprint. Only the chosen government branch is drawn.
- Expanded the tree from 54 to 121 focuses without recreating the old horizontal sprawl.

## Opening government

- Kept four explicit choices: McNutt, Harrison, Pelley, and Hapgood.
- Kept McNutt directly in power on the democratic path.
- Recast Wendell Willkie as chairman of an independent economic commission and an available advisor. No permanent McNutt-Willkie balance-of-power mechanic was added.
- Kept one temporary decision category per government, five one-time operations, and a 70 Political Control threshold.
- Added exact Political Control and secondary rewards to every decision description.
- Added actual country-tab hover biographies for McNutt, the Harrison council, Pelley, and Hapgood.

## Communist route

- The democratic opposition receives Indianapolis and approximately 55 percent of Indiana rather than only Calumet.
- Hapgood begins with two militia divisions.
- Four preparation operations can add up to six more formations, equipment, ideology support, army experience, and war support.
- The postwar focus remains unavailable until the civil war ends.

## The open files, 1936-37

- Replaced the short post-takeover cleanup with three parallel tracks the player may order freely.
- Departmental authority now requires an inventory before commissions and payroll orders are reissued.
- Terre Haute now receives a civil commission before the emergency order is withdrawn.
- Consortium Credit now has a three-step investigation through bank transfers, utility accounts, and a second lender schedule.
- The final Consortium result remains government-specific: disclose, charter, seize, or nationalize.
- Leadership changes no longer remove Indiana's inherited penalties.

## Hammond, 1937-38

- The severe Federal Embargo becomes a Federal Standoff only after all three domestic files are answered.
- Added four optional preparations: Calumet fieldworks, a State Line regiment, Monon-yard stockpiles, and orders designed to make Washington move first.
- Added a limited Illinois-Calumet border conflict against four federal emergency brigades.
- The conflict transfers no territory and lasts at least thirty days.
- Indiana victory gives the United States the Lost Indiana Operation penalty for 1,460 days.
- Indiana defeat applies Federal Supervision for 720 days; an engine-cancelled battle resolves through a 365-day compromise.

## The opened tree

- Built a Purdue instrument-and-atom arc through the Van de Graaff tower, cyclotron, Lark-Horovitz's department, instrument shops, and isotope committee.
- Built Indiana industry around Boone County electrification, the Monon and Pennsylvania, Ball and Lilly, Studebaker, Allison, Gary, Evansville, and state purchasing.
- Added Hagenbeck-Wallace winter quarters, circus trains, and Circus City as a Peru logistics and civic mini-arc.
- Built the Guard through the 38th Division, Culver Military Academy, the Black Horse Troop, Fort Benjamin Harrison, Atterbury, Baer Field, and Jefferson Proving Ground.
- Expanded Special Services into a state-police index, railroad listening posts, and an Office of Strategic Inquiry.
- Expanded diplomacy through Great Lakes trade, the American Legion headquarters question, missions, embassies, and an Indiana League.
- Added eight-focus post-emergency arcs for McNutt, Harrison, Pelley, and Hapgood, each with a meaningful fork and consolidation endpoint.
- Kept Special Services tied to the Chicago ledger.
- Moved the first Purdue nuclear material behind the completed Hammond confrontation.

## Rewards and scenes

- Added visible political power, stability, ideology, equipment, experience, and research rewards to early intermediate focuses that previously produced only events.
- Added 14 historical/alternate-history events and 27 durable national spirits for the expanded branches.
- Added Pelley's existing base-game portrait and ensured each political character has hover biography localization.
- Added a 1.19 compatibility alias for the retired unsuffixed treaty-event picture used by inherited scenes.

## Writing and presentation

- Removed player-facing recaps about crises, routes, chapters, and implementation state.
- Rewrote McNutt, McHale, and Willkie material so their responsibilities and relationship are immediately legible.
- Shortened or replaced lore that did not affect the action in front of the player.
- Preserved direct mechanical tooltips where the stakes need to be known before committing.
- Rebalanced focus pacing so the succession occupies 1936, domestic settlement fills 1936-37, and Hammond normally arrives in 1938.
