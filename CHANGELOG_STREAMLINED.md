# Four-Path Consolidation Update

## Rebuilt opener

- Added a fourth route that lets McNutt and the Indiana Democrats keep power.
- Kept Wendell Willkie as a distinct advisor and political ally rather than adding a permanent balance-of-power screen.
- Preserved the separate Harrison Crown, Pelley, and Hapgood routes.
- Replaced sequential gate decisions with four route-specific consolidation campaigns.
- Added five one-time operations to each route.
- Added one shared Political Control threshold of 70 for the final takeover.
- Limited the opener to one visible Indiana decision category at a time.

## Route changes

- McNutt now wins a state convention with support from county chairmen, precinct captains, and Willkie.
- Harrison builds financial guarantees, a public petition, and Guard oaths.
- Pelley copies the county index, compromises the Machine, tailors propaganda, and replaces the Statehouse watch.
- Hapgood rebuilds relief committees, railroad cells, arms caches, and Calumet militia.

## Civil war

- Increased the democratic opposition from approximately 35% to 55% of Indiana.
- Forced the opposition capital to Central Indiana so it receives Indianapolis.
- Gave the Wabash government two base militia divisions.
- Added up to six more militia divisions from communist preparation operations.
- Kept the postwar crisis branch locked until the civil war ends.

## Crises and story

- Made the Federal Embargo substantially more punishing.
- Political takeovers no longer clear the opening crises.
- Added a separate branch for the Vigo occupation, departmental commissions, and Chicago ledger.
- Added four route-specific Consortium outcomes.
- The embargo becomes a lighter Federal Standoff only after the domestic crisis is settled.
- Purdue remains gated behind political victory, domestic settlement, and the freight confrontation.
