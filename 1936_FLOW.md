# Streamlined Opening Flow

## Shared beginning

Indiana starts with four legible national spirits:

- A State in Suspension
- The Wabash Emergency
- The Federal Embargo
- Consortium Credit

There is no active decision category before the player chooses a route.

`A State in Suspension` is the only common political focus. It leads directly to three mutually exclusive commitments.

## Harrison Crown

1. The Harrison Offer
2. Accounts Behind the Flag
3. Decision: Commit the Guarantors
4. The Harrison Name
5. Decision: Put the Petition in Writing
6. Orders to the Adjutant General
7. Decision: Secure the Statehouse
8. An American Crown

Result: the Harrison Regency Council takes power without a civil war. Indiana becomes Non-Aligned.

## Pelley seizure

1. The Silver Entry
2. A Letter from Asheville
3. Decision: Copy the County Index
4. A Sermon for Indianapolis
5. Decision: Bind the County Chairmen
6. The Night Detail
7. Decision: Replace the Night Watch
8. The Silver State

Result: William Dudley Pelley captures the government through the Machine's administrative channels. Indiana becomes Fascist without a civil war.

## Wabash revolt

1. The Wabash Answer
2. The House on North Eighth
3. Decision: Reopen the Relief Kitchens
4. The Mine Roads
5. Decision: Move Arms Through the Mine Roads
6. The Calumet Vote
7. Decision: Call the Wabash Congress
8. The Wabash Congress
9. Win the Wabash Civil War

Result: Powers Hapgood's Communist government must defeat the Democratic opposition before the route can continue.

## Shared handoff

`A Government Washington Can Name` ends the political opener. It removes the starting political and financial crisis spirits and transforms the Federal Embargo into the simpler Federal Standoff.

Three shared follow-ups then appear:

- The Special Services Section
- Whispers from West Lafayette
- Keep the Furnaces Lit

The Purdue branch proceeds from `Whispers from West Lafayette` to `The West Lafayette Memorandum`. Its final choice ends the Federal Standoff and grants a limited nuclear research advantage.
