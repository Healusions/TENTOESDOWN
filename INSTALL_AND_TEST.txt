TENTOESDOWN - MEAT AND BONES TREE REBUILD

INSTALL

1. Close Hearts of Iron IV.
2. Commit or back up the current TENTOESDOWN folder.
3. Open the supplied ZIP.
4. Open the folder named TENTOESDOWN.
5. Copy everything inside it into the existing TENTOESDOWN mod folder.
6. Merge folders and replace files when Windows asks.
7. Start a new 1936 game. Old saves are not compatible.

The package is cumulative. Do not install an older update first.


QUICK SCRIPT TEST

1. Start Indiana on 1 January 1936.

Expected:
- Four opening national spirits.
- No Indiana decision category before choosing a government.
- An optional two-page briefing.
- Hovering over McNutt in the country screen shows a full biography.

2. Open the console and enter:

Focus.AutoComplete
Decision.Fast
event IND_debug.1

Choose the test-resources option. It also sets Political Control to 100.

3. Complete A State in Suspension and choose Keep the Indiana Democrats.

Expected:
- The other three political choices become unavailable.
- Exactly one Indiana category appears: The State Convention.
- Every decision description lists Political Control and all secondary rewards.

4. Complete the democratic focus chain and McNutt Holds the Statehouse.

Expected:
- McNutt remains Governor.
- Willkie becomes an available advisor after Bring Willkie Home.
- The campaign category disappears.
- The State in Suspension, Wabash Emergency, Federal Embargo, and Consortium Credit spirits are not removed by the convention.

5. Complete The Files Remain Open.

Expected:
- Department, Vigo, and Consortium tracks become available together.
- They can be taken in any order.
- The first focus in each track provides information or a modest benefit; it does not settle the file.

6. Complete all three tracks.

Expected:
- Reissue the Payroll Orders removes A State in Suspension.
- Close the Vigo File removes the Wabash Emergency.
- Open the Chicago Ledger removes Consortium Credit and adds The Disclosed Consortium.
- Indiana Signs Its Own Orders becomes available only after all three.

7. Complete Contest the Freight Holds.

Expected:
- The Federal Embargo becomes the Federal Standoff.
- Four optional Hammond preparations appear.
- The Hammond Inspection can be taken without them; completing them first grants the benefits shown in their focus effects.

8. Complete all four preparations, move Indiana divisions into Calumet, and complete The Hammond Inspection.

Expected:
- The confirmation tooltip states that no territory changes hands and four federal brigades are committed.
- A border conflict begins on the Illinois-Calumet line.
- The battle cannot resolve in less than thirty days.
- Indiana victory grants The Federal Line Held and gives the United States The Lost Indiana Operation for 1,460 days.
- Indiana defeat grants Federal Supervision for 720 days.
- Either result permits The State on Level Ground.

The Hammond battle can also be tested immediately from event IND_debug.1. This shortcut does not provide Indiana's normal preparation units or bonuses.

9. Complete The State on Level Ground.

Expected:
- Five compact districts open together: Purdue, industry and rail, Guard and schools, the chosen government, and Special Services/diplomacy.
- Only the selected government's institutional mini-tree is visible in the center-right district.
- The visible tree spans roughly one full-screen width rather than several empty screens.
- The continuous-focus panel sits below the final campaign row and covers no focus.
- Whispers from West Lafayette was not available before this point.

10. Follow the Purdue district through The Isotope Committee.

Expected:
- The procurement inquiry reveals nuclear and accelerator work only now.
- The state program costs 75 Political Power and grants one 50 percent nuclear bonus.
- The university arrangement costs 25 Political Power, adds 2 percent stability, and grants one 25 percent nuclear bonus.
- The later focuses build the Van de Graaff tower, cyclotron, physics department, instrument shops, and an isotope committee.
- Purdue grants research capacity and bonuses, not a free weapon.

11. Sample the industrial and military mini-arcs.

Expected:
- Boone County, the Monon and Pennsylvania, Ball and Lilly, Studebaker, Allison, Gary, Evansville, and Peru all have concrete economic or logistical rewards.
- Winter Quarters at Peru leads through Circus Trains to Circus City; it is a short story rather than an isolated joke focus.
- Culver Military Academy leads through the Black Horse Troop and staff course.
- Fort Benjamin Harrison leads through a training command and Atterbury; Baer Field and Jefferson Proving Ground feed Army of Our Own.

12. Complete the visible government mini-tree.

Expected:
- McNutt, Harrison, Pelley, or Hapgood receives a unique eight-focus institutional arc in the same compact location.
- The other three governments remain hidden.
- Each route joins two preparatory lanes, presents one mutually exclusive settlement, and ends with consolidation.

13. Follow Special Services into diplomacy.

Expected:
- The State Police Index, Railroad Listening Posts, and Office of Strategic Inquiry create an intelligence progression.
- The Meridian Street event presents a real choice about the American Legion headquarters and states both effects.
- Volunteer missions and faction creation are earned near the end rather than granted at level ground.

Turn Focus.AutoComplete and Decision.Fast off by entering both commands again.


COMMUNIST ROUTE TEST

Use a fresh 1936 game.

1. Choose The Wabash Answer.
2. Complete all five campaign operations.
3. Complete The Wabash Congress.

Expected:
- Hapgood becomes leader of the communist side and has a full country-tab biography.
- The democratic opposition receives Indianapolis and approximately 55 percent of Indiana.
- The Wabash side receives two base militia divisions plus six prepared formations.
- The campaign category disappears when the war starts.
- The Files Remain Open stays unavailable until the civil war ends.


HARRISON AND PELLEY CHECKS

Harrison:
- The Continuity Campaign is the only Indiana category.
- Renew the Chicago Advance grants one off-map civilian factory and lists it in the description.
- The Harrison Regency Council takes power without a civil war and has a country-tab biography.

Pelley:
- The Silver Entry is the only Indiana category.
- The takeover occurs through the Statehouse watch without a civil war.
- Pelley becomes leader and has a country-tab biography.
