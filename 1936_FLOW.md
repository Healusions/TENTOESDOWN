# Four-Path Opening Flow

## Shared beginning

Indiana starts with four national spirits:

- A State in Suspension
- The Wabash Emergency
- The Federal Embargo
- Consortium Credit

There is no Indiana decision category before the route choice.

A State in Suspension leads to four mutually exclusive commitments:

- Keep the Indiana Democrats
- The Harrison Offer
- The Silver Entry
- The Wabash Answer

Each route starts at 10 Political Control, has five one-time operations, and requires 70 to take power. Focuses unlock operations, but no individual operation is a focus prerequisite.

## Indiana Democrats

Focus chain:

1. Keep the Indiana Democrats
2. The Machine in Daylight
3. Bring Willkie Home
4. The 1936 State Convention
5. McNutt Holds the Statehouse

Campaign: county chairmen, open Machine books, precinct captains, Willkie's support, and the convention vote.

Result: McNutt remains governor. Willkie becomes an available advisor but does not share control of the government.

## Harrison Crown

Focus chain:

1. The Harrison Offer
2. Accounts Behind the Flag
3. The Harrison Name
4. Orders to the Adjutant General
5. An American Crown

Campaign: Chicago credit, guarantors, Columbia Club subscribers, the Harrison petition, and Guard oaths.

Result: the Harrison Regency Council takes power without a civil war.

## Pelley seizure

Focus chain:

1. The Silver Entry
2. A Letter from Asheville
3. A Sermon for Indianapolis
4. The Night Detail
5. The Silver State

Campaign: copied county files, Silver lodges, a Machine clerk, tailored propaganda, and a replaced Statehouse watch.

Result: Pelley captures the government from inside without a civil war.

## Wabash revolt

Focus chain:

1. The Wabash Answer
2. The House on North Eighth
3. The Mine Roads
4. The Calumet Vote
5. The Wabash Congress

Campaign: relief kitchens, a strike bulletin, railroad cells, mine-road caches, and Calumet militia.

Result: Hapgood leads the communist government into civil war. The democratic opposition holds Indianapolis and approximately 55% of the state. The communist side receives two base militia divisions and up to six additional prepared divisions.

## The inherited emergency

Political victory unlocks The Emergency Outlives the Governor. It does not remove any starting crisis.

The player then completes three parallel files:

1. Close the Vigo File removes the Wabash Emergency.
2. Reissue the Payroll Orders removes A State in Suspension.
3. Open the Chicago Ledger resolves Consortium Credit differently for each government.

Indiana Signs Its Own Orders closes the domestic emergency.

Contest the Freight Holds replaces the severe Federal Embargo with a lighter Federal Standoff.

Only then do Special Services, Calumet industrial work, and the Purdue story become available.
