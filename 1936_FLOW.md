# Indiana Campaign Flow, 1936-38

## January 1936

Indiana opens with four national spirits: A State in Suspension, the Wabash Emergency, the Federal Embargo, and Consortium Credit. No Indiana decision category is visible.

The common focus A State in Suspension leads directly to four mutually exclusive commitments:

- Keep the Indiana Democrats
- The Harrison Offer
- The Silver Entry
- The Wabash Answer

## Spring and summer 1936: succession

Each government receives one temporary campaign category, starts at 10 Political Control, and needs 70 to complete its takeover. Five one-time operations are available over the course of its focus chain. Focuses broaden the campaign; decisions are not keys for the next focus.

### McNutt

McHale counts and reforms the party organization while Wendell Willkie returns to chair a separate economic commission. The state convention leaves McNutt in office. Willkie becomes an advisor, not a co-governor.

### Harrison

The 1816 Society identifies its guarantors, gathers Columbia Club subscriptions, circulates the Harrison petition, and obtains Guard oaths. The Harrison Regency Council takes the executive departments.

### Pelley

Pelley photographs the county index, recruits lodges, turns a Machine clerk, tailors his propaganda, and replaces the Statehouse watch. The transfer occurs without a civil war.

### Hapgood

Hapgood rebuilds relief kitchens and railroad cells, arms mine-road caches, and negotiates with Calumet. The Wabash Congress triggers a civil war. The democratic opposition receives Indianapolis and about 55 percent of Indiana; the Wabash side receives two base militia divisions plus as many as six prepared formations.

## Late 1936 through 1937: the open files

The Files Remain Open begins after the political transfer, or after the Wabash civil war ends. Three tracks can then be completed in any order.

### Departmental authority

1. Inventory the Eight Departments - identifies which officers need current commissions and grants modest political power.
2. Reissue the Payroll Orders - removes A State in Suspension.

### Vigo County

1. Send the Civil Commission - inventories prisoners, weapons, buildings, and the telephone exchange.
2. Close the Vigo File - restores civil government and removes the Wabash Emergency.

### Consortium Credit

1. Trace the Chicago Note - follows the payroll transfers.
2. Follow the Utility Accounts - identifies the shared counsel and second schedule.
3. Open the Chicago Ledger - discloses, charters, seizes, or nationalizes the credit machinery according to the government in office.

The Special Services Section may be formed after the ledger is opened. Indiana Signs Its Own Orders requires all three files.

## Late 1937 into 1938: the federal line

Contest the Freight Holds removes the severe embargo and establishes the Hammond freight board. Four optional preparations then sit beside the short path to confrontation:

- Survey the Calumet Approaches: two fort levels and a temporary defensive spirit.
- Recall the 38th: one State Line regiment, rifles, and army experience.
- Stockpile the Monon Yards: rifles, artillery, and command power.
- Make Washington Move First: stability, war support, and a temporary defensive spirit at a political cost.

The Hammond Inspection begins a limited border conflict against four federal emergency brigades. It lasts at least thirty days and transfers no territory.

- Indiana victory: permanent Federal Line Held spirit; the United States receives the four-year Lost Indiana Operation penalty.
- Indiana defeat: two years of Federal Supervision.
- Cancelled battle: one year of Federal Supervision so the campaign cannot deadlock.

The State on Level Ground follows any result.

## 1938-40: five compact districts

Level ground opens a dense second act. The branches stay beside one another instead of stretching the screen horizontally.

### Purdue and instruments

West Lafayette proceeds from the procurement memorandum through the Van de Graaff tower, cyclotron, Lark-Horovitz's department, Lafayette instrument shops, and an isotope committee. The work supplies electronics, nuclear research, a research slot, and the first lasting scientific network. It never grants a bomb by event.

### Crossroads and war industry

The industry district combines state construction with several short arcs: Boone County electrification and the Monon/Pennsylvania lines; Ball, Lilly, Studebaker, Allison, Gary, and Evansville production; and the old Hagenbeck-Wallace winter quarters at Peru. Circus train schedules become a grounded logistics lesson before Indiana attempts an integrated arsenal.

### Guard and schools

The military district rebuilds the 38th Division, uses Culver Military Academy and its Black Horse Troop as schools rather than fantasy field formations, and develops Fort Benjamin Harrison into a state training command. Atterbury, Baer Field, and Jefferson Proving Ground supply the later mobilization base. The Hoosier General Staff and Army of Our Own are earned endpoints, not opening gifts.

### The chosen government

Only one eight-focus government arc is visible. McNutt settles the line between McHale's appointments and Willkie's commissions. The Harrison regency answers the empty-throne problem. Pelley decides whether to burn the captured index or govern through it. Hapgood chooses between a state plan and sovereign locals. Every route has a short fork and a consolidation focus.

### Special Services and diplomacy

The State Police index becomes railroad listening posts and an Office of Strategic Inquiry. Consulates then lead through Great Lakes trade, the American Legion headquarters question on Meridian Street, volunteer missions, and unrecognized embassies. Indiana can form its own league only at the end of that sequence.

Most focuses cost five weeks; major endpoints cost seven. The whole district is a 1938-40 field of choices, not a checklist intended for one year.
