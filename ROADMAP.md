# TENTOESDOWN — Roadmap

## Current vertical slice

The current build contains one complete opening loop:

- Optional two-page historical briefing.
- One common focus.
- Three explicit mutually exclusive faction routes.
- One sequential Political Crisis category with no meter.
- Harrison's regency takeover.
- Pelley's subversive seizure.
- Hapgood's civil-war trigger and postwar gate.
- A shared government handoff.
- First Special Services, Calumet conversion, and Purdue content.
- Purdue resolution of the Federal Standoff.

## Next development target

Test and stabilize this vertical slice before expanding it. The first post-test package should deepen the 1937–39 campaign without adding a new permanent dashboard:

1. Route-specific Guard command and mobilization.
2. Gary, Allison, Studebaker, and rail conversion with actual equipment and factories.
3. Special Services operations that produce concrete intelligence or map consequences.
4. A limited federal-intervention warning and defensive preparation chapter.
5. The second Purdue chapter: accelerator completion, isotope work, and the industrial requirements of a strategic program.

## Deferred content

- Full coronation and Harrison succession.
- Pelley's internal faction conflict after the seizure.
- Communist postwar reconstruction and rival labor organizations.
- Route-specific diplomacy and general war.
- Custom portraits, focus icons, event pictures, and route flags.
- Neighboring map expansion only when a finished war system requires it.

## Development rule

Build vertically. A chapter is complete only when its focuses, events, decisions, localization, AI behavior, effects, error-log state, and pacing work together. Do not add a second crisis system until the current system transforms the campaign and disappears.
